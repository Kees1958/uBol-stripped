# UBLOCK DYNAMIC — ad blocker

uBLOCK DYNAMIC — SMART, LEAN AD AND TRACKER BLOCKER

Blocks advertisements and  trackers. Your browser stays fast and does this without requiring user script permissions.  Secure by default. Want to know what happens behind the scenes of a website. Tick "I am an advanced user" in Options to show the power tools.

It brings AdGuard's power with the speed of uBlock Origin Lite. And it adds tools you won't find in other adblockers. Seamless surfing, Ad leftover cleaner, Cookie consent clicker, DNR tracking filter and Fingerprint Inspector.


SEAMLESS SURFING

Seamless surfing tries to click away cookie banners for you and turns on extra anti-tracking filters. It is on from the start. You choose the answer: accept, decline, or accept where it can and decline everywhere else. Inspired by Ghostery's Never-Consent.


COOKIE CONSENT CLICKER

Did a cookie prompt slipped through? Click its button once. The extension remembers. Next visit, the cookie prompt is gone. Inspired by Consent-O-Matic.


AD LEFTOVER CLEANER

Blocked advertisements can leave empty boxes behind. Ad leftover cleans them with one clock. See what it removed. Undo it, or keep it for that site. Powered by the general hiding rules of EasyList and AdGuard only applied smart per website.


COSMETIC ELEMENT PICKER

uBlock Origin's element picker (only with a better explanation at start). Point at anything annoying. Hide  empty ad place holders missed by the adblock filters and Ad leftover cleaner.


DNR TRACKING FILTER

Decide which third parties a site may load. To help you decide, it shows whether this domain is linsted in the DuckDuckGo tarcker databas. Block or allow everything from a domain, or only its scripts and frames. One switch shows what is blocked and what is allowed. Inspired by uBlock Origin's Mv2 classic (dynamic filtering) matrix.


FINGERPRINT INSPECTOR

See how a site tries to fingerprint you. Block each technique with one click, or pick a level: low breakage risk, medium or all. Inspired by the work of the JShelter project, only applied on-demand per domain to prevent undesired by effects (and keep browsing fast).


IMPORT ABP RULES

Paste your own filter rules, up to 5,000. Works with uBlock Origin Lite and AdGuard scriptlets,  EXCEPT scriptlets requiring trust, accepting raw javascript or long input parameters (can all be misused). Modelled on AdGuard's user rules (and recently copied by uBOL :-).


MANAGE CUSTOM RULES

All the rules created by the power tools in one place: hiding cosmetic elements, cookie clicks, DNR tracking filter and fingerprint blocks. Type a domain to see only its rules.


FILTER (BLOCK) LISTS

Only well maintained lists. AdGuard Base for ads. Peter Low's for trackers and wel curated additional anti-tracking lists. anti-adblock and anti-Admiral lists. Country lists from AdGuard and EasyList are combined and switch on automatically to match your browser's language. Add or remove them as you like.


ALLOW (WHITE) LIST

Trust a site? Paste its domain. Filtering stops there. Based on AdGuard's allowlist.


OPTIONS

Clear history, cookies and more at browser startup. When you clear cookies on start and block third-party cookies in your browser, you don´t need to worry about tracking through cookies anymore.
